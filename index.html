Your desktop already knows
what day it is.

DayWidget floats one word — today's day — over everything else. No date. No weather. No widgets stacked on widgets. Just TUESDAY, or whatever day it actually is.

Download for Windows
One .exe. No installer, no admin rights, nothing running in the background but a 30-second clock check.

What it actually looks like

Two real desktops, two real Tuesdays. No mockups — these are straight screenshots from people running it.

always-on-top · floating
next to the clock, out of the way

Built to disappear

Everything about it is designed to be forgotten — until you glance up and need it.

Practically weightless

About 3–8 MB of RAM and close to 0% CPU. It wakes up once every 30 seconds to check the day, then goes right back to sleep.

Stays where you put it

Drag it anywhere. Toggle "always on top" or "click-through" from the right-click menu. Position and settings save themselves.

Bring your own font

Loads the Anurati font privately, just for itself, from its own folder. Nothing installs system-wide — no admin rights, ever.

Never breaks

No font on hand? It quietly falls back to Segoe UI Bold. You'll never open a blank or broken window.

Lives in the tray

A small icon sits next to the clock, so you can always find or right-click it — even when it's set to click-through.

One file, no dependencies

Pure Win32 API, statically linked. No .NET, no installer, no runtime to download before it'll run.

Set up in three steps

No installer, no terminal, no account. Just a folder.

01

Download & unzip

Grab Antify.zip using the download button above and unzip it anywhere — your desktop, Documents, wherever's easy to find.

02
optional

Add the Anurati font

Anurati is free but it can't be bundled inside the zip. Download Anurati-Regular and drop it in the same folder as DayWidget.exe. Skip this and the widget still runs fine — it just uses Segoe UI Bold instead.

dafont.com/anurati → fontspace.com/anurati →
03

Double-click DayWidget.exe

The current day appears near your top-right corner, floating over your desktop. Left-click and drag to move it. Right-click for the menu — always-on-top, click-through, or exit.

＋
optional

Start it automatically with Windows

Press Win + R, type shell:startup, hit enter. Drop a shortcut to DayWidget.exe into that folder — it'll launch quietly every time you log in.

Want to change something?

Text size, color, position defaults, the 30-second update interval — it's all in one file, no build system to fight. Recompile with MinGW-w64 or MSVC's cl.exe, roughly: gcc -O2 -mwindows -municode
