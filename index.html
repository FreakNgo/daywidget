The day, on your desktop.

DayWidget is a lightweight Windows widget that puts the day of the week right on your wallpaper, in clean, oversized type you can read at a glance.

Make it yours

Your type

Pick a font or add your own, then switch bold and italic on or off. Choose the day format and letter case.

Size, spacing and opacity

Three sliders let you scale the day name, loosen the letters and fade it into the wallpaper.

Colour

Seven preset colours, or open the custom picker for an exact match to your wallpaper.

Position

Lock it in place so clicks pass straight through. Centre it or reset its position in one click.

Behaviour

Keep it above other apps, hide it during fullscreen apps, and start it automatically with Windows.

Always in the tray

Closing the settings window keeps DayWidget running. Press Ctrl+Alt+D to hide or show the widget.
